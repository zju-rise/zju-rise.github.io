### Appointment

Hundred Talents Program Researcher and PhD Supervisor, College of Computer
Science and Technology, Zhejiang University.

### Education

- **PhD, Electrical and Computer Engineering**, Georgia Institute of Technology, USA.
- **MS, Electrical and Computer Engineering**, Georgia Institute of Technology, USA.
- **BS, Computer Science and Technology**, Zhejiang University, China.

### Research Directions

Embodied AI Security · AI Security · Quantitative Finance.

### Honors and Research Funding

- Selected for the **Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas)**.
- Recipient of the **Chinese Government Award for Outstanding Self-Financed Students Abroad**.
- Principal investigator on multiple national-level research projects, including
  projects supported by the Technology Area Fund of the Basic Strengthening
  Program and the National Natural Science Foundation of China's Young Scientists Fund.

### Publications

More than 20 papers published over the past five years in leading conferences
and journals, including IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy (IEEE S&P),
USENIX Security, NDSS, ICLR, ASE, ISSTA, and IEEE Transactions on Dependable and
Secure Computing (TDSC).

### Academic Service

Journal reviewing and conference reviewing or program committee work for IEEE
Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS), IEEE Transactions on
Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC), ACM CCS, USENIX Security, NDSS, and ICLR.
