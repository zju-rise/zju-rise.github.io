I work on the security of intelligent systems, with a particular interest in AI agents and embodied intelligence. My research explores what happens when systems encounter untrusted information, adversarial collaborators, or unexpected changes in the physical environment.

Our group connects ideas from systems security, machine learning, and robotics. We develop threat models that make assumptions explicit, build reproducible evaluations, and study defenses in the context of complete workflows rather than isolated model predictions.

I enjoy working with students who are curious about how systems behave in practice and are willing to test intuitive ideas through careful experiments. At RISE, we value open discussion, clear writing, reproducible research, and collaboration across disciplinary boundaries.
