title: "Embodied AI Security"

overview: >-
  Embodied AI security examines intelligent systems that perceive, make
  decisions, and act in the physical world. The focus spans adversarial threats
  to perception, language-guided planning, and robotic control, with attention
  to how digital vulnerabilities can lead to unsafe physical behavior.
topics:
  - "Adversarial perception: evaluating the effects of manipulated visual inputs, sensor signals, and environmental cues on scene understanding and action selection."
  - "Vision-language-action model security: examining how adversarial observations and instructions influence robotic planning and execution."
  - "Secure control and physical interaction: investigating how compromised decisions propagate to robot motion, manipulation, and interactions with people or objects."
  - "Simulation and real-world evaluation: assessing attack transfer, defense robustness, and safety constraints across changing environments and operating conditions."
pipeline: >-
  Sensing → Perception → Planning → Control → Physical interaction.
  Security analysis follows the full feedback loop between intelligent systems
  and the surrounding environment.
perspective: >-
  Evaluation connects attack conditions with task performance and physical
  consequences. The goal is robust, secure operation that respects safety
  constraints under adversarial interference and environmental uncertainty.
